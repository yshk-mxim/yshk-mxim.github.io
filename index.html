Jev and Three Types of Decisions

September 24, 2026·LinkedIn·5 min read

A test of TypeSafe's Jev decision model against the game-theory benchmark from How to Fake a Theory of Mind. Jev is near-perfect on dominant-strategy games but largely ignores the opponent's payoffs, and scores 30% on second-order false-belief stories while reporting over 70% confidence. It is fast and cheap for scoring options, but it is not a trustworthy decision system.
